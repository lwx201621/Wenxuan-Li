Apr 2026 — Present Independent research Multiple datasets

Secondary data analysis of large-scale public datasets

Independent research experience

❓ The question

How can large public datasets support fairer, more transferable decisions about children’s learning and development?

🛠️ What I did

  • CFPS temporal transfer and fairness: built a seven-wave CFPS dataset (2010–2022) and six adjacent-wave prediction tasks; evaluated ML/DL models for temporal transfer, urban–rural fairness, robustness, and SHAP interpretability.
  • MICS6 early childhood targeting: harmonized child and geospatial data from six MICS6 countries (N = 33,751; 5,252 clusters), reconstructed ECDI2030 outcomes, and assessed calibration, cross-country transportability, and risk-versus-responsiveness targeting.
  • OECD IELS 2025 profile stability: rebuilt official IELS 2025 public-use data for 16,512 children across six jurisdictions; fitted K = 4–6 latent profile models across five plausible-value sets and evaluated profile- and child-level stability using Hungarian matching, ARI, posterior probabilities, and stronger-start confirmation.
  • Social-robot conditions and language: parsed 1,919 task records from 303 children in the Ohio Child Speech Corpus; derived four language outcomes and used mixed-effects models to compare language production across four robot conditions.

Dec 2024 — Sep 2026 Research assistant Henan University

Child development and nature-based education

Supervisors: Prof. Xiaomin Li & Prof. Beijia Tan

❓ The question

How do children’s relationships with nature interact with family and classroom environments over time?

🛠️ What I did

  • Managed and harmonized four-wave longitudinal data from over 1,100 children and families.
  • Independently conducted and evaluated 20+ structural equation models, including CLPM, RI-CLPM, LGCM, mediation, and moderation models.
  • Contributed to the preparation and submission of six manuscripts arising from the project, including four as second author and two as third author.
