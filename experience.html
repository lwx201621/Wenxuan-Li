Apr 2026 — Present Independent research CFPS 2010–2022

Secondary data analysis of large-scale public datasets

Independent research experience

The question

How can large public datasets support fairer, more transferable decisions about children’s learning and development?

What I did

  • CFPS temporal transfer and fairness: built a seven-wave dataset and six adjacent-wave prediction tasks; evaluated ML/DL models for transfer, urban–rural fairness, robustness, and SHAP interpretability.
  • MICS6 early childhood targeting: harmonized child and geospatial data from six countries (N = 33,751; 5,252 clusters) and assessed calibration, cross-country transportability, and risk-versus-responsiveness targeting.
  • Social-robot conditions and language: parsed 1,919 task records from 303 children and used mixed-effects models to compare language production across four robot conditions.
Dec 2024 — Jul 2026 Research assistant Henan University

Child development and nature-based education

Supervisors: Prof. Xiaomin Li & Prof. Beijia Tan

The question

How do children’s relationships with nature interact with family and classroom environments over time?

What I did

  • Managed longitudinal questionnaire data from more than 500 children and families.
  • Synthesized over 100 studies and contributed to theory development.
  • Conducted mediation, moderation, CLPM, and LGCM analyses in SPSS and Mplus.
  • Specified, compared, and refined more than 10 longitudinal model specifications.
  • Integrated child- and classroom-level datasets and supported manuscript drafting and revision.
